---

### What I've built

**Signal-Based Prospecting Engine**
Every morning, it sends a client a short list of companies that have the problem they solve right now, the people to contact, and the reason to reach out.
- For a B2B data-quality consultancy, the first run went from 1,610 job posts to 149 relevant ones to 48 companies worth contacting
- Found 68 decision-makers from just 8 searches
- Also used to find about 40 guests a week for an invite-only B2B marketing evening in Delhi

**Content Suggester**
Finds the online conversations a client's buyers are having each week, so the client knows what to reply to and what to write about next.

**[Quartermaster](https://quartermasterlabs.com/)**
A free tool that turns raw market data into clean, ready-to-use risk numbers, with a built-in check that stops bad data before it reaches you. [Code →]((https://github.com/priyanshsx/job-alerts))
